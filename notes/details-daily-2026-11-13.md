### deeprelay
- Details: DeepRelay is an inference relay that puts 48 open models behind a single OpenAI-compatible API key: chat models from DeepSeek, GLM, Kimi and Qwen, plus image and video generation, all reachable at api.deeprelay.ai with the same request shapes clients already use, including streaming, tool calls, JSON mode and reasoning tokens surfaced in usage. Instead of per-token billing only, it sells flat monthly plans — Base at $0.99 a month for 500M input and 50M output tokens, Plus at $4.99 for five times that — with usage past the allowance billed pay-as-you-go at published per-model rates, and a rolling 7-day cap alongside the monthly allowance. Each plan model carries a published usage factor, so a token on a high-factor model draws more than one token of allowance, and cached input bills at the model's cached rate. The service also publishes weights-verification results: an open-source harness samples the same prompts from DeepRelay and from independent deployments of the same published weights and runs Model Equality Testing on the completions.

### fireworks-ai-confirms-wider-breach-affecting-customer-datasets-model-weights-and-notion-ai-chats
- Details: The Hacker News reports that Fireworks AI's October 2026 security incident was broader than first disclosed: attackers reached customer account and billing information, secrets, datasets and incomplete model weights for a limited set of accounts, after reaching Fireworks' development environment through a third-party cloud provider on October 23. Notion disclosed that the incident impacted fewer than 1% of its workspaces, with Notion AI chat transcripts used for its Insights feature among the exposed data — mostly unattributable, without workspace or author labels and without tool call results. Notion has disabled Insights, stopped serving open-weight models through Fireworks, and advised affected customers to review the impacted data inside their workspace and rotate any passwords, API keys or credentials pasted into Notion AI during the exposure window.
