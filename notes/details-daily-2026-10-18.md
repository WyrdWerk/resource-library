### xplabs-experiential
- Details: XPLabs, branded "Experiential", is a small inference provider whose public models page lists per-token prices for a range of models: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (1017), GLM 5.3, Kimi K3, GPT-6 Luna, Claude Sonnet 5, Gemini 3.8 Flash, and others, alongside free tiers for several of them. The share that surfaced it announced a free uncensored DeepSeek V4.1 Flash tier at roughly 41M shared tokens per minute and ~403 TPS peak. It is worth checking for cheap or free access to recent open-weight models, but treat it as experimental: the operator announced the free tier as "free until someone stops me", so availability and pricing carry no guarantee, and the provider's uptime track record is unknown.
