# Research notes: CheapInfra #share-tech, weeks of 3–10 and 10–17 Sept 2026

Source: CheapInfra Discord server, #share-tech channel. Collected 24 Sept 2026 via
Discord search (`has:link` with weekly after:/before: ranges, scoped to channel).
Read-only collection; nothing was posted or reacted to.

Methodology note: Discord's `after:`/`before:` filters exclude the boundary dates,
so messages from the boundary days (3, 10, 17 Sept) can fall between weekly batches.

## Filter policy (set by the user)
Keep only links that count as resources: AI tools, general tools, useful websites/webpages,
GitHub repositories — things that are actually useful. Drop hype-only posts, exact
duplicates, and redundant announcement tweets when the underlying resource is already present.
Every kept link gets a ~2-sentence brief. Claims from announcement posts are flagged as
unverified where the description originates only from the poster's words.
