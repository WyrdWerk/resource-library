### 1. dquickts
- URL: https://github.com/stencil-hq/dquickts
- Posted by: Tom, 23 Oct
- Category: github, dev-tool
- Brief: dquickts is a QuickJS fork adding durable execution: suspend a running JS program mid-loop, serialize the whole heap to bytes, and resume it in another process. It also runs TypeScript natively (no transpile step), ships a durable REPL whose session survives restarts, and includes Rust bindings.
