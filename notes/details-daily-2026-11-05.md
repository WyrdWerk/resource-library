### autoloops
- Details: Autoloops is an inference provider serving open-weight models through a single API, aimed at realtime voice and agent workloads. Its catalog includes DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (552B MoE), Gemma 4 31B and 26B, and Qwen3.8-27B, alongside Qwen3-ASR speech-to-text and text-to-speech models, plus "Jev" variants that return calibrated answers. The site advertises DeepSeek V4.1 Flash at 50% off, new accounts get $1 of free credit, and dedicated deployments are offered for heavier use. The Discord sharer reports roughly 300 tokens/sec on DeepSeek with a 2 requests/second limit and about $7 per billion tokens — those figures come from the sharer's messages and are not stated on the provider's site.

### mistral-pricing
- Details: Mistral's pricing page covers Mistral Vibe, its personal AI agent, and API access in one place. Vibe runs from a free tier through Pro at $14.99 a month and Team at $24.99 per user a month to custom Enterprise, with monthly API credits bundled into the paid tiers and higher limits, storage, and support as you go up. Vibe works on web, in a terminal-native CLI, as VS Code and JetBrains plugins, and on mobile, with chat, voice mode, web search, file libraries, task scheduling, spreadsheets, slides, workflows, and connectors. The page is the reference for comparing Vibe plans side by side and for checking what the free tier includes before paying.
