## Details

### prime-intellect-inference
- Details: Prime Intellect's inference platform comes in two flavors: Prime Hosted Models (starting with GLM-5.3) served on Prime's own infrastructure, and a Prime Inference Gateway that routes to third-party models — both accessed through one OpenAI-compatible API, with a dedicated-inference option for teams that need custom latency and reliability. The company is backed by Founders Fund, NVIDIA, Intel, and angels including Andrej Karpathy and John Schulman. In the channel, one_phantomic reported their routing harness "isn't half bad" after trying it. Caveat: the originally shared link was a login-walled dashboard deep link; the public homepage documents the inference offering.

### experiential-labs
- Details: Experiential Labs is an inference gateway that puts every hosted provider behind one OpenAI-compatible /v1 endpoint, billing everything in credits at a flat 1¢ per credit with 0% markup on routed tokens — what you pay for is the plan's unlocks, not a percentage of traffic. Free gives 500 credits a month after a one-time $1 card verification; Pro ($20/mo) adds BYOK and local models, zero data retention, and "unlimited" usage of DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, GPT-6 Luna, Claude Haiku 5.5, and Jev capped at the plan's first 2,000 credits each month. Higher Max and Ultra tiers raise rate limits and deepen per-model discounts; the gateway also ships self-hostable via uvx, with budgets, per-key model allowlists, provider failover, and per-token usage attribution. Caveat: channel members challenged the "unlimited" wording as misleading, and all pricing claims are vendor-reported.
