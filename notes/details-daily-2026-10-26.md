## Details

### hemmingway
- Details: Hemmingway is an AI writing tool built around its own Hemmingway-1.5 model, designed to produce emails, texts, replies and stories that read as if a person wrote them, with a chat interface on web, Mac, Windows and Android. The vendor claims the model passes GPTZero and other top AI detectors 90 percent of the time on its own test prompts, against 86 percent for real human writing on the same detectors, and publishes side-by-side examples against other models. It is free to try without an account or card, with paid plans from 9 dollars a month for a much larger allowance, and subscribers can plug it into any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Caveats: the detector-pass figures are the vendor's own test results, not independent verification, and the free allowance is small.

### deepseek-benchmarks-deepseek-api-vs-opencode-go-vs-commandcode-goat
- Details: DeepSeek Benchmarks is a demo archive for a three-way comparison of the same coding challenge across DeepSeek Platform, OpenCode Go and CommandCode GOAT, run by Naz and shared in the channel on 25 October 2026. The site presents the original demos as submitted and lets the reader pick a round, then step inside each contender's result to compare them side by side. It extends an earlier DeepSeek-versus-OpenCode Go head-to-head with a third contender and a browsable archive rather than a single verdict thread. Caveats: these are single-user, non-deterministic demo runs rather than reproducible benchmarks, and the comparison thread itself notes the CommandCode GOAT run failed repeatedly before producing working content.
