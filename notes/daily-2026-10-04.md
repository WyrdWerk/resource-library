- Posted by: simply_roo, 4 Oct
- Category: web-app, dev-tool
- Brief: "Unlimited free EAS-compatible builds on GitHub" — `rnd build` / `rnd submit` take the same commands and flags as Expo's eas-cli, reading `eas.json` unchanged, but every build runs as a GitHub Actions workflow in your own repo (signing keys in repo secrets, no Expo account or build queue).
