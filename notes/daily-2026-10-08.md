### 2. One
- URL: https://getone.one/
- Posted by: Tom, 7 Oct
- Category: web-app, ai-tool

### 3. procedural-film
- URL: https://github.com/kuhnhomeuk-cell/procedural-film
- Posted by: Tom, 7 Oct
- Category: github, ai-tool
- Brief: An agent skill that turns a topic into a short vertical film or even a playable NES-style platformer, every pixel drawn in vanilla JS canvas and every sound synthesized in Web Audio — zero media assets. The monarch-butterfly reference film and the playable Claude Quest game demo the pipeline; expect heavy agent usage (one agent per shot, critic waves per shot). MIT licensed; ~500 stars.

### 4. orbs
- URL: https://github.com/nikuscs/orbs
- Posted by: Tom, 7 Oct
- Category: github, ai-tool
- Brief: Extracted from Pedro Martins' 7 Oct announcement tweet (tweets are never dropped for being announcements): a self-hosted multi-bot group chat where an LLM judge (Jev) routes each turn to the right bot and a daemon on your machine runs every turn through Pi (Codex, Claude, Grok, OpenRouter models).
