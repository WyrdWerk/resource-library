### openrouter-deepseek-v4-1-flash-1017
- Details: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 is the October 17, 2026 snapshot of DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, a sparse mixture-of-experts model built on DeepSeek's Causal Encoder-Decoder architecture, served under the deepseek-flash API model name with a 1,048,576-token context window, tool calling, and structured outputs. OpenRouter lists 40 providers serving it with Balanced, Nitro, and Floor routing modes plus automatic failover when a provider errors, and the page compares live per-provider pricing (from $0.006/$0.50 per million input/output tokens), throughput, latency, and uptime, alongside GPQA Diamond and TAU-Bench scores per provider. It is most useful as a live comparison for picking the cheapest or most reliable host for this snapshot: check the availability column before choosing, since Felix flagged abnormal error rates and a few providers show availability as low as 38%, and the figures shift as providers are re-tested.
