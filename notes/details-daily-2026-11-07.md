## Details

### botperch
- Details: Botperch is a plain-language guide to finding a home for an AI agent, covering 29 cloud sandbox, VM, and server providers with sourced prices, capability checks, and a cost calculator that shows its arithmetic. It walks through what actually matters per provider: isolation (container, gVisor, microVM, or full VM), sizes and regions, stop/pause/sleep lifecycle, snapshots and storage persistence, idle timers and wake paths, network access and secrets handling, billing models, and trials. Its headline finding is that the model dwarfs the machine: for a personal agent the cheapest machine is $2.98 a month while the model runs $7.30 to $488.07 a month at the site's assumed 144 million tokens, so choosing the model moves the bill far more than choosing the host. Caveat: the site is still a work in progress, machine prices were checked 29 October 2026 and model prices are medians from TokenWatch read 31 October 2026, so treat figures as list-rate illustrations and verify current pricing before deciding.

### ds-bot
- Details: DS Bot is a free, open-source (Apache 2.0) desktop app for Windows, macOS, and Linux that runs a team of long-lived bots, each with its own name, role, model, and persistent memory, that split work in group chats, hand jobs to each other, and run scheduled tasks once, daily, weekly, or every few hours. Bots share MCP servers and skills across the team, and each bot gets its own access level (read-only, workspace-only, or full access), with local commands requiring approval and token usage tracked by time, bot, and model. Everything is local-first: chats, memory, and files stay on your computer, and bots run on models you set up on your own quota, via a DeepSeek sign-in or other model services. It also works as a plugin inside DSH Desktop. Caveat: version 0.2.7 (3 November 2026) ships unsigned installers, so Windows and macOS need a manual bypass on first open.
