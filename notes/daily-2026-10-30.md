- Posted by: cata, 29 Oct
- Category: ai-tool, github
- Brief: Ovis is a free, open-source browser and phone client for herdr (formerly the herdr web UI) that turns the coding agents on a user's computer into chats answerable with a click or tap, with a live terminal, workspaces across machines over SSH, and push alerts when an agent asks something or finishes.
