## Details

### multica
- Details: Multica is a source-available workspace where AI coding agents show up on a shared board as teammates: you assign them issues the way you would a colleague, they pick the work up, comment as they go, and hand it back for review, with nothing shipping without a human sign-off. It drives the agent CLIs you already have installed — Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Kimi, OpenCode and 20 more — on runtimes you control, so code never leaves your machines, and it adds squads, skills playbooks, execution logs, usage analytics and review gates around them. You can use the hosted cloud, the desktop app, or self-host the whole stack with Docker Compose or Helm on your own infrastructure; a self-hosted server sends one anonymous daily deployment snapshot unless DO_NOT_TRACK=1 is set. The project is large and active, with 54,393 stars and 5,833 commits since January 2026, though GitHub lists its license field as NOASSERTION, so check the LICENSE file before assuming standard open-source terms.

### subvocal
- Details: Subvocal is a pair of smart glasses from The Subvocal Company that decodes deliberate silent mouthing into text at a claimed 200+ words per minute, letting you type, message, take notes or talk to AI assistants without speaking aloud or touching a keyboard. It uses muscle-sensing hardware rather than cameras or lip reading, needs no webcam, and is designed as a general input method that works in the apps you already use — ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Slack, email and documents — including dictating to coding agents next to coworkers. The YC Fall 2026 startup raised $1.3M from Y Combinator and Afore Capital, is taking US reservations at a $499 early-bird price with a refundable $199 deposit, and expects shipping to begin in Q1 2027. The 200+ WPM figure is the company's own prototype claim and has not been independently verified, and first-time setup involves a short personal calibration.
