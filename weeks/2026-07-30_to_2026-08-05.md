## Antidoom (Liquid AI blog)
- URL: https://www.liquid.ai/blog/antidoom
- Shared by: whaaaat (3 Aug)
- Categories: article
- Brief: Liquid AI's method for killing the repetitive 'doom loops' that plague small reasoning models: Final Token Preference Optimization trains the model to prefer coherent alternatives at the exact token where a loop begins, leaving the rest of the distribution untouched. On an early LFM2.5-2.6B checkpoint it cut loop rates from 10.2% to 1.4%, and on Qwen3.5-4B from 22.9% to 1% under greedy sampling — with the generation, detection, and training code open-sourced. Results are vendor-reported on vendor checkpoints and not independently verified.

## Kimi K3 on MI355X (Wafer blog)
- URL: https://www.wafer.ai/blog/kimi-k3-mi355x
- Shared by: simply soy (2 Aug)
- Categories: article
- Brief: Wafer benchmarked Moonshot's 2.8T-parameter Kimi K3 on AMD's MI355X (288GB VRAM per GPU): 952 tok/s/node aggregate and 118 tok/s single-stream — over 3.8× the per-node throughput of a 2-node B200 deployment. With the MI355X ~2.4× cheaper per GPU than NVIDIA's B300, it wins decisively on performance per dollar; the post also details real ROCm fixes, including speculative decode via RadixArk's DSpark draft and zero-padding heads to unlock the AITER MLA prefill kernel. All throughput figures are vendor-reported and not independently verified.
