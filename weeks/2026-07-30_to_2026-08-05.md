## Snowflow
- URL: https://snowflow-lilac.vercel.app/
- Shared by: Tom (30 Jul)
- Categories: web-app
- Brief: The live demo of the snowflow_demo repo below: real-time procedural snow rendering in the browser via WebGPU, Babylon.js, and hand-written WGSL — GPU-generated terrain, snow deformation, procedural characters, cloth, and post-processing with zero textures, meshes, HDRIs, or animation assets.
