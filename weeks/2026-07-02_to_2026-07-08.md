## tailscale-shell
- URL: https://github.com/monotykamary/tailscale-shell
- Shared by: Tom (4 Jul)
- Categories: dev-tool, github
- Brief: A zsh-based proxied shell plus exit-node picker for Tailscale userspace networking: pick an exit node by country, city, or code and route egress through Mullvad or your own relay via SOCKS5. Small repo (~4 stars), but a practical wrapper around the tailscale CLI.

## archify
- URL: https://github.com/tt-a1i/archify
