## Dukoi
- URL: https://dukoi.com/
- Shared by: thelaggingway (13 Nov)
- Categories: dev-tool, web-app
- Brief: Dukoi turns a spare Android phone into a server — install the app, pair it with the CLI over Wi-Fi, and deploy real apps that get a live URL, with no root, no Termux, and no cloud bill. It runs apps as a foreground service that restarts them within seconds if Android kills them, works behind CGNAT with optional custom domains via Cloudflare, and keeps every deploy as a rollback-able release with live logs in the terminal and dashboard.

## Zeron
- URL: https://zeron.sh/
- Shared by: ROman (14 Nov)
- Categories: ai-tool, dev-tool
- Brief: Zeron is a native desktop app for driving the coding agents already on your machine — Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Grok, Hermes, and Pi — from any of your devices, with sessions living on your machines and every device able to pilot them. Start a session at your desk, watch it from your phone, and approve diffs from the couch, with plugins and a chart-drawing reply view built in.
