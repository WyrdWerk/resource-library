## boxd

- Categories: dev-tool, web-app
- Brief: Boxd offers "composable computers": persistent KVM dev VMs (2 vCPU, 8 GB RAM, 100 GB disk by default) with live memory forking in under 200ms, snapshots, checkpoints, per-machine HTTPS subdomains, and a CLI/API plus MCP integrations for GitHub, Linear, and Slack — pitched for giving agents root and walking away.
