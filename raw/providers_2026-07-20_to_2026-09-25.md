search snippets where pages were rate-limited/JS-heavy, marked as such).

third-party allegations seen during research (OpenFerence, Crof) are NOT
published as facts. A public API key exposed in two Zro embeds on 2026-09-03
was deliberately never reproduced.

oldest-first, falling back to bare-name keyword) recorded the first mention:
date, sharer, message gist. Four batches covered all 81 kept entries; all
dates fall within 2026-07-20 → 2026-09-25. Two entries needed follow-up
keyword searches (Reflex: 2026-08-21 Alp; BytePlus ModelArk: 2026-09-14
Rykuuun). StreamLake, CoreWeave, Parasail, GMI Cloud, Venice and Inceptron
